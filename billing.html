From first step to delivery

Know what you pay.
Before we build.

  1. Start with the free OS

    Ask about access to the base platform. No custom implementation purchase is required.

  2. Agree on the scope

    Confirm deliverables, prerequisites, price and any recurring or usage-based costs in writing.

  3. Approve and pay

    The $499 promotion and fixed starter packages are paid upfront. Custom projects use agreed milestones, typically 50% / 30% / 20%.

  4. Launch and expand

    Test the agreed deliverables. Additional work needs a separate approved quote.
