You retain ownership of the business data and content you provide to Rocket Club. We use it to answer requests and perform the services you approve. You may email us to request deletion. Some records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, security or dispute-resolution purposes.

This policy covers the Rocket Club website and services unless a signed agreement or data-processing addendum provides more specific terms.

1. Who we are and scope

Rocket Club LLC (“Rocket Club,” “we,” “us”) is a New Jersey limited liability company in the United States. This policy describes how we process information through this website, business communications, AI implementation services and managed services. For customer data processed on a business customer's instructions, the customer may act as controller/business and our signed agreement or data-processing addendum governs our processing.

2. Information we collect

Quote and contact forms may collect your name, business email, phone, company and website, industry, team size, selected services, current tools, workflow description, message, consent, and information you include in a request. The form and website may also transmit the page or referring address, campaign parameters, and technical data needed for operation, security, and troubleshooting. If you become a customer, we may process project data, authorized integration data, account and billing records, and service-usage information as described in your agreement. Payment-card details are intended to be handled by the applicable payment provider, not stored by Rocket Club.

3. Sources

We collect information directly from you or your organization, from systems you authorize, automatically through website operation and security logs, from service providers, or from public business sources where permitted by law.

4. How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, prepare a requested plan or proposal, communicate about a request, configure and deliver contracted services, secure and troubleshoot systems, administer accounts, measure separately authorized AI Cloud usage, process transactions through a payment provider, prevent fraud, maintain business records, comply with law, and communicate service or policy updates. Submitting a quote request does not authorize paid work or an AI Cloud charge.

5. Customer data ownership and use

As between Rocket Club and a customer, the customer retains ownership of the customer's business data, content and materials. Rocket Club accesses and processes customer data only as reasonably needed to respond to an authorized request or provide the agreed service, protect systems, and meet legal obligations. We do not use customer confidential data to train a general-purpose model unless the customer expressly authorizes that use in a signed writing. The customer's agreement may describe approved AI and infrastructure providers for a specific service.

6. How information is shared

We may share information with hosting, communications, security, payment, scheduling, customer-management, and AI providers when needed for the requested or contracted function; professional advisers; authorities where legally required; a successor in a merger, reorganization, or sale; and other parties at your direction. Information included in a contracted AI workflow may be transmitted to the AI or infrastructure providers selected for that service. Provider identity, processing location, retention, and model settings depend on the approved architecture and agreement. Do not submit health, financial-account, biometric, government-ID, children's, or other sensitive personal data unless the written scope and safeguards expressly permit it.

You may request deletion of information Rocket Club controls by emailing rocketclub23@gmail.com with “Data deletion request” in the subject. We will delete or de-identify information we control when appropriate, except information we must or may retain for legal, tax, accounting, security, dispute-resolution, fraud-prevention, or contract-administration purposes. Copies in routine backups may remain until those backups are overwritten. A customer asking us to delete data processed for its service must also ensure deletion in systems and third-party accounts the customer controls. A deletion request does not itself cancel a paid service or waive outstanding contractual charges.
