Plain-language summary

We collect only information reasonably needed to respond to inquiries, provide contracted services, secure our systems and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information for money.

This policy covers the Rocket Club website and services unless a signed agreement says otherwise.

1. Scope and responsible company

This Privacy Policy describes how Rocket Club and the Rocket Club operating entity identified on an applicable proposal, order form or invoice (collectively, “Rocket Club,” “we,” “us”) process personal information through this website, business communications, AI implementation services and managed cloud services. A customer may act as the data controller or business for information it provides to us; in that case, our signed agreement and any data processing addendum control.

2. Information we collect

We may collect contact and business information, including name, business email, phone number, company, industry, job role and website; inquiry and project information submitted through forms or communications; transaction information such as purchased service, payment status and billing identifiers; usage and device information such as IP address, browser, pages visited, timestamps and security logs; and customer content or authorized integration data required to provide contracted services. Payment card details are processed by payment providers such as Stripe and are not intended to be stored on Rocket Club systems.

3. Sources

Information may come directly from you or your organization, from your authorized systems and integrations, automatically from your use of the website, from service providers, or from publicly available business sources where permitted by law.

4. How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries; schedule calls and demonstrations; prepare proposals; configure, deliver, support and improve services; authenticate users; measure authorized AI Cloud usage; process transactions; prevent fraud and security incidents; maintain business records; comply with law; and communicate service or policy updates. We do not use customer confidential data to train a general-purpose model unless a signed agreement expressly authorizes it.

5. Legal grounds

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information to perform a contract or take requested pre-contract steps, pursue legitimate business and security interests, comply with law, or act with consent. Consent may be withdrawn where consent is the basis, without affecting earlier lawful processing.

6. How information is disclosed

We may disclose information to cloud hosting, communications, analytics, security, payment, scheduling, customer-management and AI model providers acting for us; professional advisers; authorities where legally required; a successor in a merger, financing, reorganization or sale; and other parties at your direction. Providers receive information only as reasonably necessary for their function and are expected to protect it. We do not disclose customer confidential information for third-party targeted advertising.

7. AI providers and customer content

Authorized content may be transmitted to selected AI or infrastructure providers to generate requested outputs. Provider identity, data location, retention and training settings may vary by selected architecture and contract. Customers must not submit regulated, highly sensitive or third-party information unless the applicable scope and safeguards expressly permit it.

8. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described, including service delivery, account administration, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Retention depends on information type, contract requirements, sensitivity and applicable limitation periods. Information may persist temporarily in secure backups before deletion cycles complete.

9. Security

We use administrative, technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the service and information. No transmission, storage system or AI service can be guaranteed completely secure. Customers are responsible for access controls, credentials, lawful data collection and security of systems outside Rocket Club’s control.

10. Privacy choices and rights

Depending on location and applicable thresholds, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or information about disclosures. We may verify identity and authority, retain information allowed or required by law, and deny requests where an exemption applies. Authorized agents may be required to provide proof of authority. Submit a request through our contact page with “Privacy Request” in the message.

11. Cookies and signals

We may use essential storage for security and functionality and, if enabled, limited analytics to understand website performance. See our Cookie Notice. Because no universal browser-based “Do Not Track” standard is currently binding across all contexts, our response may vary; we will honor legally required opt-out preference signals where applicable.

12. Children

The website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website. Contact us if you believe a child submitted information.

13. International processing

Information may be processed in the United States and other locations used by approved providers. Where required, we use contractual or other recognized safeguards for cross-border transfers.

14. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as services or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the latest version. Material changes will be communicated where required. Privacy questions and requests may be submitted through our contact page.
