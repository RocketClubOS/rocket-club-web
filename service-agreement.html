This page summarizes standard business-client terms; the signed agreement and project SOW control.

1. Company and business services

These standard policies apply to business AI strategy, agents, software, integrations, websites, APIs, cloud, database, authentication, and related administration or maintenance provided by Rocket Club LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company. They are for business customers and do not remove any rights that applicable law makes non-waivable.

2. Written scope and changing prices

All website prices are starting estimates, not a final offer. Prices can change based on the business, locations, workforce, workflows, channels, data, security needs, third-party systems, integrations, and the agreed scope of work. The final price is the one written in the SOW and accepted by both parties. Any change to scope, price, delivery, or included support requires written approval.

3. Signing and when implementation starts

The customer and Rocket Club sign the same SOW and service agreement before work begins. The Effective Date is the date of the last signature. The customer then has five consecutive calendar days to cancel as described below. Rocket Club does not begin implementation during those five days. Implementation automatically begins at 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the sixth calendar day after the Effective Date.

4. Five-day cancellation and full refund

The customer may cancel the signed project without cause by emailing rocketclub23@gmail.com before 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the fifth calendar day after the Effective Date. The request must identify the customer and project. Rocket Club will refund 100% of all installation amounts it received for that SOW, including any tax collected on those amounts where applicable, to the original payment method. Payment-provider processing times may vary. Statutory cancellation rights that provide more protection remain in effect.

5. Cancellation after implementation begins

After the five-day period ends, installation fees already paid are nonrefundable, except where applicable law requires otherwise. The customer may still cancel implementation by written notice to rocketclub23@gmail.com. If the customer cancels for convenience after the five-day window, it owes no remaining unpaid fixed project or installation balance; Rocket Club waives that future balance. Separately authorized AI Cloud usage already incurred and third-party charges expressly approved in the SOW remain payable. Cancellation does not give the customer ownership or a right to use incomplete work in progress. The customer keeps its own data, pre-existing materials, domains, and accounts.

6. Who owns the data and deliverables?

The customer always owns its business data, customer records, brand, content, domains, and materials it supplied. Rocket Club uses that information only to respond to the request or deliver the agreed service, protect systems, and meet legal obligations. A customer may request deletion of information Rocket Club controls by emailing rocketclub23@gmail.com. Legal records and routine backups may need to be retained for a limited period.

Once the SOW is completed and all amounts due for it are paid, ownership of the custom deliverables listed in that SOW transfers to the customer. This can include the agreed website or web application, custom code, API work, database schema and configuration, and authentication configuration. Rocket Club retains its reusable frameworks, tools, generic components, and know-how. Third-party and open-source components remain subject to their own licenses.

If the customer cancels after implementation begins but before the project is completed, unfinished and undelivered work remains Rocket Club's property; the customer receives no license to copy, deploy, or have another provider use that incomplete work. Rocket Club may remove incomplete implementation from systems it controls. This does not take ownership of customer data, pre-existing materials, domains, or accounts. Ending a separate maintenance service later does not undo ownership of deliverables already transferred after project completion and payment.

7. Administration and maintenance

Where included in an active SOW, Rocket Club administers and maintains the customer's website, web application, API, cloud environment, database, and authentication configuration as a service provider—not as their owner. Accounts should be created in the customer's name wherever the third-party provider allows. The customer remains the account owner and pays third-party bills unless the SOW says otherwise. Rocket Club's administrative access is limited to the agreed work and ends or changes when the applicable service ends. Ongoing maintenance, hosting, monitoring, backups, support hours, and response targets are included only if the SOW lists them.

8. AI Cloud and third-party charges

AI Cloud usage, model/API requests, messages, hosting, storage, and other provider costs are separate from installation unless the SOW explicitly includes them. Before activation, the SOW must disclose rates, units, billing frequency, included usage, limits, overages, and cancellation terms. A quote request does not authorize a payment or recurring charge.

9. Client responsibilities and AI limitations

The customer supplies authorized data and access, appoints a decision-maker, reviews the work, and remains responsible for its business decisions and lawful use. AI output can be inaccurate, biased, incomplete, or unavailable. Human review is required for consequential decisions, including hiring and workforce actions. Rocket Club does not promise particular revenue, savings, conversion, accuracy, or business results unless a specific measurable commitment appears in the signed SOW.

10. Disputes, law, and contact

New Jersey law governs the service agreement, subject to non-waivable law. The parties will first try to resolve a dispute through their authorized business contacts. Subject to applicable law, court venue is Bergen County, New Jersey. Nothing here prevents a lawful complaint, payment-provider dispute, or right that cannot be waived.
