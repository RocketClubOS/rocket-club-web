## 1. Documents and order of precedence

This Agreement consists of these terms and the Statement of Work or order form signed by both Parties at the same time (the **SOW**). The SOW must identify the specific service and its scope, deliverables, dependencies, schedule, fees, payment milestones, acceptance criteria, support, third-party costs, taxes, AI Cloud usage, customer-owned accounts, and any expressly agreed exceptions. The Agreement and SOW take effect on the date of the last signature. That date starts the five-calendar-day cancellation period in Section 5. Rocket Club will not begin implementation during that period. Implementation automatically begins at 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the sixth calendar day after the Effective Date unless Client timely cancels. Website estimates and demonstrations are not offers or commitments. An SOW controls over this Agreement only for a specific conflict it identifies.

## 2. Services, scope, and changes

Rocket Club will provide only the services and deliverables described in an approved SOW. All website prices are starting estimates, subject to change based on Client's business, locations, workforce, workflows, data, channels, integrations, security requirements, third-party systems, and final scope. Only the final price written in the approved SOW is binding.

Either Party may request a change. A change affecting scope, fees, assumptions, deliverables, timing, or acceptance is not effective until both Parties approve a written change order. Rocket Club is not required to begin extra work before that approval.

## 3. Client responsibilities and schedule

Client will appoint an authorized project owner; provide accurate requirements and timely decisions; obtain all permissions, notices, consents, and rights needed for Client Data and connected accounts; provide secure, least-privilege access and personnel for testing; and review outputs before using them in production. Client controls its business decisions, customer communications, employment decisions, regulated activities, and final use of deliverables.

The schedule in an SOW assumes Client meets its dependencies on time. If a dependency, approval, access credential, or third-party service is unavailable, the Parties will document a reasonable schedule adjustment; material additional effort requires an approved change order.

## 4. Fees, taxes, and payment

The SOW will state fees, currency, invoice dates, payment milestones, taxes, and any third-party charges. Unless the SOW expressly says otherwise, taxes, third-party licenses, paid model/API usage, hosting, messaging, and AI Cloud consumption are not included in the one-time installation fee. Rocket Club will disclose usage units, rates, billing frequency, included amount, limits, and overage rules in the SOW before activating metered or recurring usage. Client does not authorize undisclosed recurring or usage charges by signing this Agreement alone.

Client will pay undisputed invoices by the due date in the SOW. The Parties will promptly raise and work in good faith to resolve invoice disputes. Any late fee, suspension right, or collection cost must be stated in the SOW and comply with applicable law.

## 5. Cancellation and refunds

**Five-day cancellation window:** Client may cancel without cause by emailing **rocketclub23@gmail.com** before 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the fifth calendar day after the Effective Date. The request must identify the Client and SOW. Rocket Club will refund 100% of installation fees received under that SOW to the original payment method; payment-provider processing time may vary. If this deadline falls on a weekend or holiday, the five calendar days still run unless applicable law requires more time. Rights that cannot legally be waived remain unaffected.

**After the five-day window:** Implementation automatically begins at 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on calendar day six. Client may terminate implementation at any time by written notice to **rocketclub23@gmail.com**. Installation fees already paid are nonrefundable except where applicable law requires otherwise. Upon Client's convenience termination after the window, Client owes no remaining unpaid fixed project/installation balance, and Rocket Club waives that future balance. This waiver does not cover separately authorized AI Cloud usage already incurred or third-party commitments Client expressly approved in the SOW. The termination and ownership consequences in Sections 9 and 15 apply.

**AI Cloud and recurring services:** The SOW must specify how to stop future recurring charges and how already-consumed usage is measured and invoiced. Cancellation does not waive non-waivable rights or charges properly incurred before the effective cancellation date.

## 6. Acceptance and remediation

The SOW will state objective acceptance criteria and a review period. If none is stated, Client will have ten business days after written delivery to identify a material failure to meet the SOW in a sufficiently detailed notice. Rocket Club will use commercially reasonable efforts to correct a timely reported, reproducible failure within the agreed scope. If it cannot do so after a reasonable opportunity, the Parties will apply any non-refund remedy expressly stated in the SOW, subject to applicable law. No refund is promised after implementation begins except where applicable law requires it or an SOW expressly grants that remedy. Acceptance does not waive latent defects or rights that cannot legally be waived.

## 7. Customer data, privacy, and deletion

**Client Data** means information, content, prompts, records, credentials, and materials supplied by or for Client or obtained from Client's authorized systems. As between the Parties, Client retains all rights and ownership in Client Data. Client grants Rocket Club a limited, nonexclusive right to access, host, transmit, and process Client Data only to perform the SOW, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. Rocket Club will not use Client Data to train a general-purpose AI model unless Client separately authorizes that use in a signed writing.

Rocket Club will use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the agreed service. No system or AI service can be guaranteed completely secure. Client will not submit sensitive, regulated, biometric, health, financial-account, or third-party personal data unless the SOW identifies the purpose, roles, approved providers, safeguards, and applicable data-processing terms.

Client may request deletion of Client Data by emailing **rocketclub23@gmail.com**. After service termination or a verified request, Rocket Club will delete or return Client Data it controls as agreed in the SOW, except records it must retain by law or for legitimate tax, accounting, security, dispute-resolution, or contract-administration purposes. Data in routine backups may remain until overwritten. Client remains responsible for deleting data in accounts and systems Client controls. A data processing addendum takes precedence for personal data it covers.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each Party will use the other Party's nonpublic information only for this Agreement and protect it with at least reasonable care. The duty does not cover information the receiving Party can show it lawfully knew without restriction, developed independently, received lawfully from another source, or that became public without breach. A Party may disclose information where legally compelled, giving advance notice when legally permitted. These duties continue for **[three (3) years]** after disclosure; trade secrets remain protected while they qualify as trade secrets under applicable law.

## 9. Ownership, administration, and intellectual property

Client always owns Client Data, its brands, content, domains, and materials it supplied before the project. Client-owned cloud, database, API, authentication, hosting, and website accounts must be opened in Client's name where the provider permits it; Rocket Club receives only the administrative permissions reasonably needed to build, operate, maintain, or support the systems described in the SOW. Client remains the account owner and is responsible for third-party subscription charges unless the SOW says otherwise. Rocket Club will not claim ownership of Client's accounts or Client Data.

**If the project is completed and both Parties have performed their obligations:** Client owns the bespoke deliverables expressly listed in the SOW—including the agreed website/web application, custom source code, API components, database schema/configuration, and authentication configuration—once the contracted scope is completed and all amounts due for that SOW are paid. Rocket Club then acts only as administrator/maintenance provider to the extent a separate active support or maintenance scope authorizes access. Ownership does not include Rocket Club's pre-existing tools, reusable frameworks, generic components, skills, or improvements, nor third-party/open-source materials subject to their own licenses. Any embedded Rocket Club background technology is licensed to Client perpetually, worldwide, and without additional fee solely as needed to use the paid deliverables; counsel must confirm this license and assignment wording.

**If Client cancels after the five-day window:** No title, assignment, or license to incomplete work-in-progress or undelivered project deliverables transfers to Client. Rocket Club retains those materials and may disable or remove the incomplete implementation from Rocket Club-controlled environments. Client may not use, copy, deploy, or have another provider use those incomplete deliverables. This does not transfer, restrict, or erase Client Data, Client's own pre-existing materials, domains, or accounts. Rights that cannot lawfully be waived remain unaffected.

Rocket Club does not provide legal, tax, employment, financial, or medical advice.
