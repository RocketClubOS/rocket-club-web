Effective August 17, 2026

Core principle

AI assists people. The customer remains responsible for reviewing outputs and decisions affecting customers, employees, finances, health, legal rights or safety.

1. AI limitations

AI systems are probabilistic. Outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, biased or inappropriate. They are not guaranteed facts, professional advice or a substitute for qualified judgment.

2. Human oversight

Customers must maintain meaningful human review for material decisions and escalation paths for uncertain, sensitive or high-impact matters. AI may not be the sole decision-maker for employment, credit, insurance, housing, healthcare, legal services or other legally protected access or eligibility decisions unless expressly permitted by law and a signed scope with appropriate safeguards.

3. Authorized data

Customers may submit only data they are authorized to process. Regulated health, financial, biometric, children’s, government identifier or similarly sensitive data requires prior written approval, appropriate architecture and any required agreement. Do not place passwords, payment card numbers or authentication secrets into prompts.

4. Models and providers

Rocket Club may use third-party model and infrastructure providers identified by architecture or order form. Models and results may change. We may replace providers for security, availability, quality or compliance reasons while seeking materially comparable functionality.

5. AI Cloud usage

Installation fees are separate from ongoing consumption. Billable units may include model input/output tokens, requests, voice or processing minutes, storage, messages, searches, integrations and provider charges. Applicable rates, included allowances and overage treatment must be disclosed before recurring billing begins. Estimates are not guarantees of future consumption.

6. Controls

Available controls may include usage limits, alerts, access roles, logging, content filters and human approval. The customer is responsible for selecting limits appropriate to its risk and promptly reporting suspected abuse or incorrect behavior.

7. Prohibited use

Services may not be used for unlawful surveillance, deceptive impersonation, fraud, malware, exploitation, discrimination, harassment, evasion of safety controls or content that violates applicable law or third-party rights. Digital twins require authorization from the represented person and may not be presented deceptively.

8. Outputs and records

Customers must independently verify important outputs and preserve records required for their industry. Rocket Club does not guarantee that AI-generated records satisfy a regulatory retention, audit or evidentiary requirement unless expressly included in a signed compliance scope.

9. Suspension

Rocket Club may restrict an integration or workflow where continued operation presents a credible security, legal, safety or provider-policy risk, and will provide notice when reasonably possible.

10. Relationship to other terms

These terms supplement the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and signed customer agreement. Signed terms control in a conflict.
