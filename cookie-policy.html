Cookie Notice

Effective August 17, 2026

Current approach

Rocket Club aims to minimize browser storage. We do not use cookies to sell personal information or run third-party behavioral advertising through this website.

1. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies are small identifiers or local storage mechanisms used by websites to remember information, maintain security and understand performance.

2. Essential technologies

Essential storage may be used for security, fraud prevention, session continuity, accessibility preferences, payment flow and other functions requested by the visitor. Disabling essential technology may prevent parts of the website from working.

3. Analytics

If analytics are enabled, we may collect limited device, referral and interaction information to understand aggregate website performance. We will update this notice and provide consent controls where applicable before introducing nonessential technologies that require consent.

4. Payment providers

When you proceed to a hosted payment page, Stripe or another disclosed payment provider may use its own cookies and similar technologies under its privacy terms for payment, authentication, fraud prevention and wallet eligibility.

5. Your choices

You can control cookies through browser settings. Deleting or blocking storage may reset preferences or affect functionality. Where legally required, a consent interface will allow you to reject nonessential technologies as easily as accepting them.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this notice when technologies change. Questions may be sent through our contact page. See the Privacy Policy for more information.
