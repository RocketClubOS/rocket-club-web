Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective August 17, 2026

Plain-language summary

Installation is a one-time service. AI Cloud is separate and begins only after disclosed terms and authorization. Contact us promptly if a payment appears incorrect.

1. One-time installations

A customer may request cancellation before implementation work begins. If Rocket Club has not started work, provisioned paid resources or incurred nonrecoverable provider costs, the installation payment will generally be refunded to the original payment method, less any nonrefundable charges clearly disclosed before purchase where lawful.

2. Work already started

Once discovery, configuration, design, development, provisioning or integration work begins, payments compensate reserved capacity and work performed and are nonrefundable except as required by law or stated in a signed agreement. If Rocket Club cannot deliver the agreed service for reasons within its control, the parties will first attempt correction, replacement or a fair refund for undelivered scope.

3. Custom modules

Deposits, milestones and acceptance for custom work are governed by the signed proposal or statement of work. Customer-caused delay, unavailable access, changed requirements or third-party platform restrictions do not automatically create a refund right.

4. AI Cloud and recurring services

Recurring or metered billing will not begin until material pricing, frequency, included usage and cancellation instructions are disclosed and accepted. Cancellation stops future renewal charges according to the stated effective date; already consumed usage and accrued charges remain payable. Customers should receive a cancellation method reasonably comparable in ease to enrollment.

5. Usage disputes

Report a suspected usage or billing error through the contact page within 30 days of the charge, including the invoice date and disputed amount. We will review relevant metering and provider records. This reporting period does not limit rights that cannot be waived by law.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact Rocket Club first so we can investigate. Nothing here prevents a lawful dispute with a payment provider. Fraudulent or bad-faith chargebacks may result in suspension and recovery of amounts lawfully due.

7. Processing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Bank and payment-provider processing times vary and are outside Rocket Club’s control.

8. Mandatory rights

This policy does not exclude refunds, cancellations or remedies that applicable law does not permit the parties to waive. A signed customer agreement controls where it provides additional rights.
