Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Rocket Club LLC · New Jersey, United States

Plain-language summary

Cancel the signed project within five calendar days for a full refund of installation amounts paid. After the five-day period, paid installation fees are nonrefundable, but the customer may cancel and will not owe the remaining fixed project balance, subject to applicable law.

1. Five-day cancellation period

The customer may cancel the signed installation without cause by emailing rocketclub23@gmail.com before 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the fifth calendar day after the last signature on the service agreement and SOW. Rocket Club will refund 100% of all installation amounts received under that SOW, including any tax collected on those amounts where applicable, to the original payment method. Implementation does not begin during this period. Applicable law may provide additional rights.

2. After the five-day period

Implementation begins automatically at 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the sixth calendar day after the last signature. The customer may cancel implementation in writing, but installation fees already paid are nonrefundable except where law requires otherwise. Upon a convenience cancellation, the customer owes no remaining unpaid fixed project/installation balance. Separately authorized AI Cloud usage already incurred and third-party charges expressly approved in the SOW remain payable. Cancellation does not transfer unfinished work or give the customer a license to use it. The customer's own data, pre-existing materials, domains, and accounts remain the customer's.

3. Custom modules

Deliverables and payment schedules for custom work are governed by the signed proposal or statement of work. That document must identify the implementation start date and may not remove the five-day cancellation period except as applicable law allows.

4. AI Cloud and recurring services

Recurring or metered billing will not begin until material pricing, frequency, included usage, limits or overages, and cancellation instructions are disclosed and accepted. Cancellation stops future renewal charges according to the stated effective date; properly authorized usage already consumed and accrued charges remain payable, subject to applicable law. The applicable order form must describe a cancellation method.

5. Usage disputes

Report a suspected usage or billing error through the contact page or to rocketclub23@gmail.com as soon as reasonably possible after discovery, including the invoice date and disputed amount. We will review relevant metering and provider records. This request does not limit rights that cannot be waived by law.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact Rocket Club first so we can investigate. Nothing here prevents a lawful dispute with a payment provider. Any suspension or recovery of amounts must comply with the applicable agreement and law.

7. Processing and mandatory rights

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method; bank and payment-provider processing times vary. This policy does not exclude refunds, cancellations, or remedies that applicable law does not permit the parties to waive. Contact Rocket Club LLC at rocketclub23@gmail.com.
